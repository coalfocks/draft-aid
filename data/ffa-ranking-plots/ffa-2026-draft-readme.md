# FFA 2026 Visible Rankings - 14-Team Draft Read

Source: Fantasy Football Analytics visible Shiny data and ranking plots.

Only the top 10 per position bucket were exposed without the paid CSV export. The valid downloaded ranking graphics are QB, RB, WR, and TE. The app repeated the TE plot for K/DST/IDP image downloads, so those repeated images were excluded.

## Keeper

Keep Puka Nacua for the 3rd. The FFA model has him as WR1, overall rank 7, with a very tight range: 221 floor, 233 projection, 243 ceiling. That is first-round value at a third-round cost.

## Draft Takeaways

- RB: Gibbs is the clean RB1, then Bijan/Taylor, then CMC/Henry/Cook. After Achane/Saquon/Jeanty/Jacobs, the top visible RB tier is already thinning. In a 14-team league, RB scarcity is real.
- WR: Puka and Chase sit alone at the top, JSN is next, then Amon-Ra. There is a big projection cliff from Amon-Ra at 200 to London at 179. Do not lazily assume WR is deep if using this model.
- QB: Josh Allen is the only huge gap. Lamar/Hurts/Daniels/Maye/Dart are close enough that price matters. Burrow/Caleb/Dak/Nix are viable if the room pushes QBs up too hard.
- TE: McBride and Bowers are the only obvious advantage plays. After Bowers, there is a 22-point drop to Loveland. LaPorta is TE9 here, so he is not a better keeper than Puka.
- K/DST: Aubrey grades absurdly high for a kicker, but do not spend mid-round capital unless your league scoring truly rewards it. Texans/Broncos/Seahawks are the top DST group, but streaming still beats forcing it early in most leagues.
- IDP: LB scoring looks more valuable than DL/DB in the visible slice. Maxx Crosby is the standout DL; LB has a flatter top tier; DB is very flat across the top 10.

## 14-Team Rule

Use the tiers more than the raw ranks. When a tier is about to end at RB/WR, take the scarce position. When QB/TE are in a flat cluster, wait and let someone else pay for the name.
